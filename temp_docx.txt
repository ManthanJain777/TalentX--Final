1. Project Overview
TALENTX is a talent-first recruitment platform intended to move the process from reactive applications to proactive discovery. Candidates create one verified Talent Passport; employers search and contact suitable candidates; candidates retain visibility and response control.
Candidate-first, employer-discovery model
Evidence-backed talent profiles
Explainable skill-level matching
Consent-driven visibility
Full-stack academic MVP with clear extension points
Post-match project governance through contracts, milestones, escrow, deliverables and project health.
Employer Challenges that create a practical cold-start and revenue mechanism.
Clear extension points for future verification, AI assistance and institutional integrations.
2. Problem Statement
Application black holes: candidates can submit many applications without useful feedback.
Screening overload: employers must sort large applicant pools.
Keyword filtering can miss capable candidates.
Self-reported skills lack strong evidence.
Candidates have limited control over how and when employers discover them.

Competitive framing: TALENTX sits at the intersection of LinkedIn Recruiter-style discovery, Upwork-style project execution, and Toptal-style quality signals, while integrating evidence, explainability and governed delivery in one workflow.
The differentiation claim should remain precise: TALENTX is not presented as the literal replacement for every capability of these platforms; its advantage is the integrated proof → discovery → delivery loop.
